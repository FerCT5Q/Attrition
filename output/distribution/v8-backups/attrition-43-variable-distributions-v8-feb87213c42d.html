Employee characteristics and attrition

How characteristics are distributed and how voluntary attrition differs across them

Internal analysis

What may be related
to voluntary attrition?

We reviewed employee characteristics that may help explain or be related to voluntary attrition. Each slide shows how employees are distributed across categories and, where available, the voluntary attrition rate for each category. Most distributions show employees active in August 2026. Each slide states its own time period. These comparisons show patterns; they do not prove cause.

Our workforceEmployees with Regular status who were active at the date used for each slide.
Voluntary attritionThe percentage who chose to leave the company. Other types of departure are not included.

Why 2025 appearsWe start with 45,003 Regular employees active on 24 Aug 2025 and follow them to 23 Aug 2026.
