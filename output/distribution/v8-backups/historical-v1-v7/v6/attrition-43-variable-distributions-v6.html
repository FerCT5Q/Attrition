How to read this slide
Internal aggregate analysis

Most people applied to one job

Each person is counted once per fiscal year and grouped by the number of different jobs they applied to. This includes only people found in the application records, not the full workforce.

What stands out: FY2024 had more people applying to several jobs. In FY2025 and FY2026 year to date, applications were more concentrated in one or two jobs.
How the recent-application measure would work
1
Use only information already availableIgnore applications recorded after the date being evaluated.
2
Look back six monthsCount each different job only once during that period.
3
Identify frequent activityMark “Yes” at four or more different jobs. This is separate from the annual 4+ bar above.
Important limit: This slide cannot estimate how many employees made no application. The records begin with people already linked to an application.
Internal-use limit: FY2025 and FY2026 year to date include small aggregate groups below 30 people and are shown only for the requester and Julia. No employee details, identifiers, job postings or titles are shown; no manager access, redistribution, operational use, score changes, or production approval.
