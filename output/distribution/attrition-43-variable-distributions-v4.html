How the rate is calculated

Managers are selected at the August cutoff. Voluntary exits over the prior six months are divided by the average team size across those same months.

  • All teams: 3.11 voluntary exits per 100 average direct reports
  • Teams with more than 5 reports: 2.98 per 100
  • Difference: 0.13 percentage points
  • Exit rule: Voluntary exits only; assigned to the manager before the exit

Bottom line: This describes team history. It is not a manager rating or a causal result.
